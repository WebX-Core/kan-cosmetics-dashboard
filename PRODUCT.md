# Product

## Register

product

## Users

KAN Cosmetics admin staff in Nepal: store managers, marketing leads, and superadmins. They work at a desk, in an office or stockroom context, primary on laptop/desktop. Primary jobs: manage product catalog, process orders, review customer data, and publish content. Sessions are task-driven and goal-focused — they come in to do something specific, not browse.

## Product Purpose

A full-featured e-commerce operations dashboard for KAN Cosmetics — Nepal's premier beauty brand. Covers catalog (products, categories, variants), commerce (orders, carts, coupons, payments), customers, marketing (campaigns, blog, ads), delivery logistics, and RBAC/system management. Success means the admin can run the business end-to-end without leaving the tool.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, Modern, Confident. The product should feel like it belongs to a premium beauty brand — not a generic SaaS tool wearing the brand's colors. The dark luxury visual language (deep plum/burgundy backgrounds, rose-gold accents, refined typography) must be consistent from the login screen through every dashboard surface.

## Anti-references

- Clinical white: Sterile, hospital-bright, zero warmth. Shopify admin, plain white SaaS.
- Generic product-neutral SaaS: Blue-on-white, forgettable, interchangeable with any other B2B tool.
- Loud/discounted e-commerce: Noisy, rainbow-colored, sale-banner energy.

## Design Principles

1. **Brand at every touchpoint.** The login, the dashboard shell, and every form must feel unmistakably KAN — not a white-label admin tool that happens to have a logo in the corner.
2. **Calm confidence over busyness.** Premium feels restrained. Use space deliberately; resist the urge to fill every corner with information.
3. **Dark luxury with purpose.** The dark palette is a brand choice, not just an aesthetic. Maintain it consistently; don't let individual pages drift back to generic white.
4. **Clarity serves the operator.** The brand aesthetic must never compromise task efficiency. Tables, forms, and data views prioritize legibility and action over decoration.
5. **Motion with intention.** Transitions and animations should feel fluid and considered — luxury brands don't jerk or flash. Ease out, never bounce.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. All body text and interactive controls must meet 4.5:1 contrast against their backgrounds. The dark palette requires special care: ensure muted/secondary text isn't lost against dark surfaces. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` throughout.
